Build by students, for students who build

Orbit 3.0 started as a small group of students trading notes on AI and web projects. It has grown into the department's home for technical activity — the place where a hackathon idea becomes a working prototype, and a workshop turns into a study group that keeps meeting.

We focus on AI, machine learning, computer science, programming, web development and cybersecurity, but our events are open to students from every engineering branch who want to build something.

  • Run entirely by student volunteers, with faculty and alumni as mentors
  • Events open to all branches, not just computer science
  • Everything documented and reusable for the next batch of students
